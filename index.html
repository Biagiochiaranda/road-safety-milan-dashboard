Difference between absolute volume and normalized risk

This chart compares ranking position. A negative value means the municipality ranks higher in normalized risk than in absolute accident volume.

How to read the municipality difference

Absolute ranking and normalized ranking can tell different stories. An area can have many accidents because it has more traffic, residents or daily users. Normalization divides accidents by resident population and helps compare areas on the same scale.

In this draft, Municipio 9 — Porta Garibaldi / Niguarda has the highest normalized value. This supports the idea that “many accidents” does not always mean “highest relative risk”.

4. Vehicles involved. This section shows the composition of accidents by number of vehicles involved.

Vehicle-involvement distribution, 2024

Interpretation of the pie chart

The pie chart shows how recorded accidents are distributed by number of vehicles involved. This is useful because not all accidents have the same interaction structure: some involve only one vehicle, while others involve two or more vehicles.

If the largest share is represented by two-vehicle accidents, the dashboard suggests that interaction between road users is a central component of accident dynamics.
